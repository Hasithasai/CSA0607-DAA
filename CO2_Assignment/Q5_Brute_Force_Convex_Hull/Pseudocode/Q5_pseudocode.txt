1. Remove duplicate coordinate pairs.
2. For every unordered pair of distinct points A and B:
   a. Treat AB as a candidate line.
   b. Evaluate the cross product (B-A) x (P-A) for every other point P.
   c. Record whether any point is strictly on the positive side and whether any is strictly on the negative side.
   d. If points occur on both sides, reject AB.
   e. Otherwise accept AB as a supporting boundary segment (collinear points may lie on the line).
3. Collect endpoints of accepted edges.
4. Order the boundary vertices around the perimeter for display.
5. Return the hull and operation count.
